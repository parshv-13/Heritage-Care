# Naga Morung Storytelling — Voice-First AI Cognitive Game Concept

## 📜 Concept & Gameplay
A voice-first game where an animated village elder character (set in a traditional Naga longhouse / morung) begins telling a well-known regional folk tale but pauses at key moments, asking the player to recall or supply the next part: 
> *"And what did the tiger say to the boy?"*

- **Voice-Based Interaction:** Players respond by voice in their own regional language or dialect.
- **Supportive & Non-Judgmental Logic:** Partial or approximate answers are accepted and gently built upon rather than marked wrong.
- **Personal Story Memory Archive:** Over time, the game invites players to tell their own family stories into the app. These are saved as personal "chapters" that the game can later ask them to recall, creating a running personal memory archive useful to family members and caregivers.

---

## 🧠 Cognitive & Therapeutic Impact
- **Targeted Cognitive Domains:**
  - **Semantic Memory:** Leverages culturally shared knowledge, which is often well-preserved even in moderate dementia.
  - **Narrative Sequencing:** Encourages logical order and flow during recall.
  - **Language Production:** Stimulates natural speech and vocabulary retrieval.
- **Social-Emotional Engagement:** Delivers genuine connection through storytelling, a proven non-pharmacological approach for reducing agitation and supporting identity in dementia care (**Reminiscence Therapy**).
- **Family Archive:** Recording the player's personal and family stories generates a meaningful narrative history for loved ones.

---

## 🌾 Cultural Connection (North East Region - NER Specific)
- **The Morung Institution:** Storytelling in the *morung* is a foundational Naga cultural institution — oral history, not text, is how knowledge, traditions, and values were historically passed down.
- **Linguistic Adaptability:** Folk tales can be curated per-tribe (*Ao, Angami, Sumi, Lotha, etc.*) and delivered in Nagamese or local dialects, making this one of the most culturally and linguistically adaptable concepts for North East India.

---

## 🤖 AI Adaptive Dynamics
- **Fluency & Retrieval Tracking:** Rather than tracking strict "correct/incorrect" answers, the AI evaluates:
  - **Response Latency**
  - **Coherence**
  - **Prompt Assistance Count:** Tracks whether the player needed 1, 2, or 3 rounds of gentle prompting before responding.
  - *These metrics act as a proxy for verbal fluency and retrieval difficulty over time.*
- **Adaptive Emotional Support:** If a player struggles significantly on a given day, the AI seamlessly shifts from asking-for-recall to narrating the full story warmly. This preserves the emotional and social value of the interaction even when memory retrieval is difficult that day.
